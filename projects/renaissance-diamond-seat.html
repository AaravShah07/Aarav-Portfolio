Diamond Seat Redesign

A design-for-manufacturing fix: reshaping a diamond seat to stop casting from damaging the stones.

Role
Mechanical Design Intern
Dates
June–July 2024

Design for manufacturing

Heat damage during casting

Problem. With the original seat (Fig. 2), the diamonds took thermal damage during casting, which left them foggy or blackened.

Fix. The redesign reduced the seat’s points of contact with each stone, and its new petal-shaped seat (Fig. 3) lets investment powder flow in around the stone. The casting process stayed the same; the part’s geometry changed to suit it.

Result. The fix worked in production, and Renaissance took the work forward with its engineering consulting team.

My part

The redesign was a shared effort. I contributed to it by:

  • Helping redesign the seat geometry to reduce stone contact points.
  • Working on design iterations and tolerance refinement to improve durability.
  • Working with the production team to account for manufacturability.
